\begin{center}
    \textbf{\huge{General conclusion and perspectives}}
\end{center}
\vspace{1cm}
\normalsize
\lettrine{I}n conclusion, this project was a real opportunity to appreciate and enrich my training at the National Engineering School of Sfax (ENIS) since it helped me stimulate my scientific, technological and social knowledge.
\vspace{0.5cm}
The development of this project entitled "PTSDetection" allowed me to design and implement a web application. The application offers a set of services and helps patients for the follow-up and detection of PTSD.
\vspace{0.5cm}
This work also provided me with a great contribution on several levels. On the one hand, it was the opportunity to apply the assets acquired during my years of training in Computer Engineering and on the other hand to become familiar with group work.
\vspace{0.5cm}
Another important aspect of the experience was the opportunity to organize group work using github, and to exploit the ReactJs library on the front-end and Django on the back-end. This allowed us to reinforce our development skills and have a good experience with them.
\vspace{0.5cm}
In spite of the temporary and technological constraints that I met which are summarized mainly in the new spirit of the design of the database and in the complexity of the design of the graphic interfaces, I succeeded in finishing the application with the module of the  PCL-5 test and the acquisition of the videos and the virtual interviewer.
\vspace{0.5cm}
This work has accomplished its specified objectives, but, like any other work, it cannot claim perfection. It therefore deserves some improvements. One of the possible improvements consists in integrating a module that gives the patient the hand to modify his information and to integrate several other languages besides English.
