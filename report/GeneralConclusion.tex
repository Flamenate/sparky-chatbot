\begin{center}
    \textbf{\huge{General conclusion and perspectives}}
\end{center}

This project confirmed the practical importance of intelligent conversational systems for service-oriented businesses such as Sparky Solutions Photovoltaiques. In a context where response speed strongly influences customer engagement, the chatbot offers a concrete way to reduce initial friction between customer intent and commercial action. Beyond the technical artifact itself, this work was also a major learning opportunity for our team: it allowed us to explore the full lifecycle of an LLM-based system, from prompt governance and tool-calling logic to multi-channel webhook integration, production deployment constraints, and operational safety controls.

Throughout this report, we examined the project from both analytical and practical angles: the business need, functional scope, architecture and AI design choices, and the production setup that enabled real multi-channel operation. We also documented runtime behavior through concrete workflows and interaction scenarios to show how the system performs beyond theoretical design.

The project also involved clear trade-offs. Because available proprietary data was limited, we prioritized prompt engineering and tool-calling over fine-tuning. We also favored a unified multi-channel core and strict safety policies, accepting some conservatism in responses in exchange for stronger reliability, confidentiality, and maintainability.

Looking forward, the chatbot has strong growth potential. The next steps are to improve multilingual adaptation, strengthen personalization, and enhance quote precision through richer business data integration. As more validated interactions are collected, retrieval-augmented or selective fine-tuning strategies can be explored to further improve performance under the same safety constraints.

In summary, this end-of-year project delivered a functional, business-relevant, and technically grounded AI assistant that demonstrates both immediate value and long-term extensibility. It provided a complete learning journey across requirements engineering, AI system design, implementation, and production-oriented validation, while laying a clear foundation for future iterations of a more capable and scalable commercial assistant.
